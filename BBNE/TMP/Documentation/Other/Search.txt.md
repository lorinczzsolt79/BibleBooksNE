
# Search

## How to?

### Minimum:

	1. Select the Bible!
	2. Write something to search into the text box
	3. Click on the [Search] button

### Cleansing

#### Verses

- remove {ab}.*{/ab} parts in NIV
- replace <.*> to SPACE
- <span style="color: red;">
- </span>
- replace [\d*] to SPACE

##### HKB

- <br>
- plus sign

##### NIV

- <span style="color: red;">.*</span>
- [\d*]
- [text]

#### Footnote

- < .*>		<br> ; <i></i>
- [\d*]
- [text]
